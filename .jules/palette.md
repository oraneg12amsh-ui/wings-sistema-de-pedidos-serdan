## 2024-06-12 - Missing ARIA Labels on Icon-Only Controls
**Learning:** Several icon-only buttons in `index.html` (e.g., cart quantity adjustments, modal close buttons, mobile cart toggles) lack `aria-label` attributes. These are crucial for screen reader users to understand the function of these controls.
**Action:** Always verify icon-only controls have localized (Spanish, as per the app) `aria-label`s.

## 2025-05-18 - Missing ARIA Labels on Icon Buttons\n**Learning:** The Wing's Aliens app uses multiple icon-only buttons (like close buttons with SVG or `&times;` and mobile cart buttons) without accessible labels, meaning screen reader users are completely unable to interact with or understand these key navigation controls.\n**Action:** Add `aria-label` attributes to all icon-only buttons to ensure keyboard and screen reader accessibility.
